📋 How to Play

  • Press START, then drive the truck with the arrow buttons or your keyboard arrow keys.
  • Drive onto a house to deliver water. Each delivery helps one family (+10 points).
  • Don't wait too long between houses: water spills after 1.5 seconds of driving (-1 water, -5 points).
  • Pick Easy, Normal or Hard before you start: it changes your time, water and how fast water spills. Level 1: help all 10 families in 30 seconds. Level 2: help 5 houses and the hospital in 25 seconds. The hospital needs 2 water.
